WHY I CHOSE THESE COURSES

My course logic: build a foundation, then follow what I learned from work.

  1. Years 1–2 | Turning requirements into foundationsMost management, accounting, economics, statistics, programming and database courses were required. They gave me a business vocabulary and showed me that I enjoy breaking problems down with data.
  2. Summer before Year 3 | Shihlin Electric made the direction concreteWorking in the domestic sales department made me want to understand how teams coordinate work, schedules and resources. That internship prompted me to build project and industrial management skills.
  3. Year 3 | Turning electives into a directionI then chose management information systems, operations, supply chain, project management, service management, AI forecasting and sustainable engineering, while completing the Low Carbon & Sustainability Management microprogram. My electives gradually converged around data, projects and sustainability.
