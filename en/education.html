WHY I CHOSE THESE COURSES

How my course choices evolved from Year 1 to now

I began by building a foundation in business management. The turning point came during the summer between Years 2 and 3, when I interned at Shihlin Electric.

  1. 2023.09 — 2024.06Year 1 | Learning the language of businessAccounting, management, economics, calculus and web design were mostly required. They gave me a business foundation and helped me discover that I enjoy breaking problems down through code and numbers.
  2. 2024.09 — 2025.06Year 2 | Connecting analysis with management questionsStatistics, databases, marketing, organizational behavior and management science taught me to see data, consumers and organizations together. Required courses still formed the core, while I began looking for a direction to explore further.
  3. 2025.07 — 08Summer before Year 3 | Shihlin Electric became the turning pointWorking in the domestic sales department made me curious about how teams coordinate schedules and resources. It gave me a clear reason to build project and industrial management skills.
  4. 2025.09 — 2026.06Year 3 | Making electives point in one directionManagement information systems, operations, supply chain, project management, service management, AI forecasting and sustainable engineering brought my electives together around data, projects and sustainability. I also completed the Low Carbon & Sustainability Management microprogram along the way.
  5. 2026.09 — presentYear 4 | Bringing coursework into research and practiceI now bring data analysis, AI and consumer insight into my work at MARTECH LAB 921, while continuing to turn what I learn into tangible competition and project outcomes.
