Social innovation

Family Translator: bringing generations closer

Photos and voice messages help older adults share everyday life. We explored how AI might narrow the digital distance between family members.

Event4th Future Problem-Solving Competition
When2026.07.29
My roleMember of the Translation Konjac team
RecognitionHonorable mention

How we approached the question

Survey feedback in the team report

42.2%Reported lacking conversation topics
70.3%Found photo-to-text sharing convenient
89.1%Interested or conditionally willing to try

We translated feedback into everyday postcards, cross-generational language explanations and digital help. LINE provides a familiar entry point where photos and voice can start conversations.

About the figures

These percentages are reported survey/feedback figures. Complete sample denominators are not clearly specified; they are not adoption rates or evidence of long-term impact.

What we worked on

  • Combined LINE, generative AI and voice interactions in a cross-generational communication proposal.
  • Developed everyday sharing, digital assistance and care reminders, with a deck, report and poster.
Technology can make it easier to express care, as well as save time.
