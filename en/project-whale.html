Yaoguang Whale: more independent access to services

As public kiosks shift to touchscreens, how can people with visual impairments use them independently?

EventNational Business & Interdisciplinary Innovation Project Competition
When2026.06.25
My roleTwo-person team member
RecognitionThird place · Interdisciplinary Projects

How we approached the question

Usability evaluation plan

5Evaluation dimensions
Software-ledNo hardware modifications proposed

The proposal combines NFC/BLE triggers, voice guidance and privacy-aware interactions. Planned measures include task completion, time, errors, assistance needed, and perceived safety/privacy, with simulated hospital payment and ATM interfaces.

About the figures

These are proof-of-concept plans in the deck, not completed usability test results.

What we worked on

  • Worked with Hao-Hua Hsu on an accessibility system proposed to work without hardware modifications.
  • Developed needs, interaction flows and a plan for usability validation across service settings.
