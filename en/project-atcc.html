BEACON: support where young people already are

We explored ways to make psychological support resources approachable through familiar interactive environments.

Event23rd ATCC National Business Case Competition
When2026.04–05
My roleKTV Lab team member

How we approached the question

Service architecture proposal

3 layersCapture, analysis and support routing
Online + offlineA connected service journey

The proposal connects playful interactions with pathways to support resources. Its information flow covers signals, analysis and routing to suitable support, rather than requiring a young person to begin with a formal request for help.

About the figures

Risk categories are proposed service mechanisms, not clinical diagnoses. The official record confirms advancement to the preliminary final.

What we worked on

  • Developed BEACON for a challenge involving the Lin Yu-Lin Hong Tai Education Foundation and Teacher Chang Foundation.
  • Connected Roblox, a LINE alternate-reality game and physical interactions.
  • Prepared a proposal, a site-visit presentation and the BEACON pitch.
A helpful service also needs to appear somewhere people feel comfortable approaching it.
