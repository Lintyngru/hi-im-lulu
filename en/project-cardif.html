Insurance Bus: protection in everyday moments

Starting with Gen Z life situations, we designed an approachable insurance journey on LINE.

When2026.01.25

RecognitionSilver award

How we approached the question

Proposal targets

50,000Target LINE friends
NT$2.4MProposed revenue target
Life eventsEntry point to insurance

Life events trigger protection needs. Quizzes, membership segments, points and subscriptions connect awareness, interaction, conversion and retention in a trackable customer journey.

About the figures

The friend count and revenue are targets in the proposal, not results of an operating service.

What we worked on

  • Designed a situational LINE entry point, interactive quiz, recommendations and membership journey.
  • Led the integration of strategy, content structure and the pitch deck.
  • Explored subscriptions, loyalty points, segmented CRM and behavior tracking.
