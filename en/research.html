RESEARCH 01 · UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH PROJECT

Designing AI that supports comfortable decisions.

Undergraduate research project · overall review grade A (top 20%)

Formal research title

Designing Conversational AI for Comfortable Decisions: The Role of Reciprocity, Personalization, and Companionship Mechanisms across Generations

Conversational AIDecision comfortCASA2 × 2 scenario experimentCross-generational
01

Why this study

Generative AI is becoming a partner in complex choices, yet much of the literature focuses on accuracy and efficiency. This study asks whether an AI conversation can also help people feel more comfortable making a decision.

02

Research approach

Grounded in CASA theory, the 2 × 2 scenario experiment manipulates reciprocity and personalized responses. It examines para-social friendship, social support and flow as paths to decision comfort, with comparisons across Generations X, Y and Z.

03

Current status

Research in progress

The research question, theoretical framework and experimental design are complete, and the proposal received an overall A rating (top 20%). Data collection and analysis will follow, so no empirical conclusion is claimed yet.

RESEARCH 02 · CAPSTONE

Understanding a mall experience through 250 reviews.

June 2026 · four-person undergraduate capstone

Formal capstone title

Applying SOR Theory to Explore the Impact of Mall Value on Consumer Behavioral Responses: A Case Study of Eslite Spectrum Xindian

Consumer behaviorS-O-R250 Google reviewsText analysisFactor & regression analysis
01

Why this study

As e-commerce grows, physical malls are becoming experiential spaces. Eslite Spectrum Xindian combines dining, brands and entertainment with natural resting areas and cultural experiences. We asked how these two kinds of value shape consumer feelings and behavior.

02

Research approach

The team cleaned and coded 250 Google reviews, transforming unstructured text into quantitative data. Using the S-O-R framework, we applied exploratory factor analysis, Pearson correlations and regression analysis.

03

What we found

  • Utilitarian value more directly predicted purchase intention (β = .235, p < .001).
  • Hedonic value significantly increased comfort and novelty; its effect on novelty was β = .290 (p < .001).
  • Novelty increased positive word of mouth (β = .294, p < .001), while hedonic value did not directly predict purchase intention (p = .231).

Research process, practical insight and limitations
  1. Collect and clean reviews
  2. Binary coding and manual review
  3. Factor, correlation and regression analysis
  4. Connect findings to consumers and spatial experiences

In practice, dining, brands and entertainment form the base for conversion, while greenery, resting areas, exhibitions and distinctive events create comfort, novelty and recommendation intent. To turn positive experiences into purchases, malls can connect them with loyalty programs, offers or limited products.

About the figures

This study analyzes 250 public reviews from one mall using binary coding for whether a topic appeared. The results describe statistical associations rather than causation and do not represent every visitor.
