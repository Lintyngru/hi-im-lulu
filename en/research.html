RESEARCH 01 · UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH PROJECT

Designing AI that supports comfortable decisions.

Undergraduate research project · overall review grade A (top 20%)

Formal research title

Designing Conversational AI for Comfortable Decisions: The Role of Reciprocity, Personalization, and Companionship Mechanisms across Generations

Conversational AIDecision comfortCASA2 × 2 scenario experimentCross-generational

I ask more than whether AI recommendations are accurate: how do responses, contextual continuity and social cues help people feel understood and supported during complex choices?

AOverall review grade
TOP 20%Review ranking band
2 × 2Scenario experiment design
Open the research design and eight review ratings

Grounded in CASA theory, the study proposes a 2 × 2 scenario experiment manipulating reciprocity and tailored response. It examines para-social friendship, social support and flow as paths to decision comfort, with generational differences across Gen X, Y and Z.
