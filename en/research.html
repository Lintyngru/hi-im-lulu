RESEARCH 01

Understanding a mall experience through 250 reviews.

June 2026 · four-person undergraduate capstone

Our capstone uses the S-O-R framework to explore relationships between mall value and consumer responses at Eslite Spectrum, Xindian Yulon City.

250Cleaned Google reviews
4Capstone team members
S-O-RTheoretical coding framework
  1. Collect and clean reviews
  2. Theory-based coding, AI assistance and manual review
  3. Factor, correlation and regression analysis
  4. Connect findings to consumers and spatial experiences

My question is: beyond convenience, how do comfort and novelty in a space relate to word of mouth and purchase intentions?

About the figures

The analysis uses 250 reviews from one mall and describes statistical associations, not causal effects.
