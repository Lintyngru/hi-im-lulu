Cypress Connections: turning footfall into encounters

We connected family needs, in-store experiences and membership through review analysis and store observations.

How we approached the question

Content analysis & service design

~350Social posts and comments
~300Family venue and dining reviews
90 secProposed in-store experience

We connected social content, venue reviews and brand observations to a smell–try–ask–recommend experience. QR interactions would capture preferences and support membership. The two datasets are different sources, not a sample of independent respondents.

About the figures

The data volumes and service flow come from our strategy proposal. Reach and revenue estimates in the attachments are projections, not achieved results.

What we worked on

  • Analyzed social content and reviews to identify family-oriented needs and opportunities.
  • Connected event traffic, hands-on experiences, QR interactions and CRM.
  • Led the team in bringing the strategy and presentation together.
Footfall does not automatically become a relationship. Every step from noticing a store to trying a product deserves thoughtful design.
